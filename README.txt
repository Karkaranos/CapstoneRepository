Wayward Woods

--Controls--

Left Click to interact
Right Click to drop held Spell/Artifact
~/` to open console
ESC to open pause menu

--Helpful Information--

You will need to equip at least 1 spell before entering combat; artifacts are optional
To cast a spell, you must click the tile an enemy is on
You can only have one spell of each type at a time

--Known Functional Bugs--
Enemies can pass through other enemies
Enemies can stand on the same tile
Enemies die before the attack VFX show
The only spell that works with multiple enemies on the same tile is Lightning 4- Lightning Strikes
Clicking on an enemy out of range to cast a spell consumes Action Points
Lightning Bounce spell does not always not work
Artifacts may be duplicated by alternating left and right click in a specific pattern
Hazards (red boxes) do not always deal damage to characters standing on them
Glass Cannon does not deal twice the damage; it just doubles the amount of damage taken
Tornado may deal damage to the player
Some spells have damage rounding errors

--Known Visual Bugs--
Trees clip
Player sprite layers over enemy sprite
Tornado VFX lasts too long

--Missing Feedback--
Action Points need more feedback
Artifacts with multiple slots do not gray out if they cannot be used
Some attacks are missing VFX
Marks need feedback- if you have 2 or 3 Artifacts with Mark of Speed, you can move up to 3 times on your turn. This is working as intended, but needs feedback
