Wayward Woods

--Controls--

Left Click to interact
~/` to open console
ESC to open pause menu
WASD to select tiles
Arrow Keys to pan the camera

--Helpful Information--
Some spells will require you to select a path before casting. Click on the player (or a highlighted tile), path out 6 tiles, and click on the ending tile
	- Tornado
	- Wind Barrier
	- Wind Current
Some spells will teleport you to a tile you select within range
	- Stormrider
	- Zapstep



--Known Functional Bugs--
- The ranged enemy has a large range



--Known Visual Bugs--
- Sprites layer incorrectly
- Damage feedback will sometimes double up
